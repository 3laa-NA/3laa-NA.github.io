Why this order differs from most guides you'll find online

The 2010 movie doesn't replace episodes 58–61. A lot of lists have you skip the Benizakura Arc on TV and watch the movie in its place. Every detailed watch-order source has you watch the full TV run and treats the movie as an optional bonus retelling, watched afterward.

The "Futon ni Haitte kara" special doesn't split Gintama° in half. It's often placed 30 episodes into the fourth season. It was actually released as a tie-in right when that season premiered — it belongs right before it starts, not in the middle.

Porori-hen airs after Gintama., not before. Some lists reorder these two for "chronology," but nothing in the show's official release or relation data supports watching Porori-hen first, so this stays in broadcast order.
