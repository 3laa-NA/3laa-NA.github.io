Jump Festa 2005 Special

33 min Sep 24, 2005

A standalone comedy OVA released before the TV series. Gintoki, Shinpachi and Kagura stumble through short comedic vignettes in an alien-occupied Edo.

Some fans recommend waiting until after ~20 episodes so you already know the characters. Either way, it's pure comedy with zero plot impact.

TV Series

Gintama (2006) — Episodes 3–57

55 episodes Apr 2006 – Mar 2007

The story truly begins here. Gintoki, Shinpachi and Kagura take odd jobs in a futuristic Edo overrun by aliens. Parody, gag humour, and the slow build of the Yorozuya family.

Skip Episodes 1–2. They're a confusing announcement special. The real story starts at Episode 3.

Gintama: The Movie — Benizakura Arc

95 min Apr 24, 2010

A high-budget remake of the Benizakura Arc. The definitive version of the story that finally kicks the serious plot into gear.

Watch this instead of Episodes 58–61. The movie retells the exact same arc with far superior animation.

What to Skip (If You Want)

Anime-Original Filler

1–2, 50, 57, 75, 106, 114, 124, 125, 135, 137, 150, 155, 164, 166, 171, 173–174, 176, 185, 209, 252

Only ~6% of Gintama is filler.

Recap Episodes

  • Episode 125 — Yorozuya / Shinsengumi recap
  • Episode 209 — Meta recap episode

Mixed Episodes

Episodes 82, 111, 120 mix canon and original content. They contain plot-relevant moments and are generally worth watching.

Final Tip

The first 20–30 episodes are largely character introductions. Push through to the Benizakura Arc where the serious plot kicks in.
